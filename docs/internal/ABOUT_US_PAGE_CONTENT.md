# About ContactIn

## Why this plugin exists
Contact forms should create clarity, not clutter. ContactIn exists to turn incoming messages into organized, actionable conversations without adding complexity to your workflow.

## Problem it solves
Most websites receive a mix of sales questions, support requests, and general inquiries in one noisy stream. That slows down response times, causes missed opportunities, and makes follow-up inconsistent. ContactIn automatically structures submissions so teams can prioritize what matters first.

## Vision
Our vision is simple: every website should have an inbox that is intelligent, fast, and easy to trust. ContactIn is built to make communication management feel lightweight while still delivering meaningful insight from every message.

## Commitment to performance & privacy
We prioritize speed, reliability, and responsible data handling. ContactIn is designed to run efficiently in real-world WordPress environments and to follow privacy-conscious practices so site owners stay in control of their data and their users' trust.
